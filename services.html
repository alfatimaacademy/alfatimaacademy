Noorani Qaida Course | Learn Quran Reading Foundation

Start your journey of learning the Holy Quran with our structured Online Noorani Qaida Course. Designed for beginners, children, and adults, this course provides a strong foundation in Arabic letter recognition, correct pronunciation, and basic Tajweed rules.

Course Overview

The Noorani Qaida is the essential first step for anyone learning to read the Quran in its original Arabic text. Our course helps students speak and recognize Arabic letters, join words, and practice correct pronunciation (Makharij) before moving on to Quran recitation (Nazra).

What You Will Learn

  • Arabic Alphabet & Pronunciation: Master all Arabic letters with correct Makharij (articulation points).
  • Letter Shapes & Joining: Recognize letters in isolated, initial, medial, and final forms.
  • Vowels & Marks (Harakat): Learn Fatha, Kasra, Damma, Tanween, and Sukun.
  • Basic Tajweed Rules: Practice Madd, Leen, Shaddah, and basic rules of recitation.
  • Fluent Word Formation: Move step by step from single letters to full Arabic words and verses.

Tajweed-ul-Quran Course | Master Quran Recitation & Pronunciation

Improve your Quran reading with our comprehensive Online Tajweed-ul-Quran Course. Designed for beginners and advanced learners alike, this course helps you master Arabic phonetics, articulation points (Makharij), and Tajweed rules to recite the Holy Quran with clarity, beauty, and confidence.

Course Overview

Proper Quran recitation requires following the established rules of Tajweed the precise pronunciation and rules governing Arabic letters. Our Tajweed course guides students step by step to eliminate common recitation mistakes, refine accents, and recite the Quran exactly as revealed to the Prophetﷺ.

What You Will Learn

  • Makharij-al-Huroof: Learn the exact articulation points for all 28 Arabic letters.
  • Core Tajweed Rules: Master essential rules such as Ghunnah, Ikhfa, Idgham, Iqlab, and Izhar.
  • Rules of Noon & Meem Sakinah: Understand correct nasalization and pauses in recitation.
  • Madd Rules (Elongation): Practice short, medium, and long vowel extensions.
  • Stop & Pause Signs (Waqf): Learn where and how to pause correctly while reading verses.
  • Vocal Control & Accent: Develop a rhythmic, melodious, and correct recitation style.

Quran Memorization (Hifz) Course | Memorize the Holy Quran Online

Start your sacred journey of memorizing the Holy Quran with our structured Online Quran Memorization Course. Designed for kids, adults, and beginners, our program provides a systematic, flexible, and supportive environment to help you memorize specific Surahs or the complete Quran with correct Tajweed.

Course Overview

Memorizing the Quran (Hifz) is a deeply rewarding spiritual goal. Our Online Hifz Program combines daily one on one lessons, customized schedules, and personalized revision plans tailored to each student's retention capacity to ensure long-term retention and accurate recitation.

What You Will Learn

  • Flexible Hifz Targets: Choose to memorize short Surahs, selected chapters (e.g., Surah Yaseen, Surah Mulk), or the full Quran.
  • Balanced Lesson Structure: Follow a daily routine divided into three key parts:

    Sabaq (New Lesson): Daily new verses assigned according to your memorization speed.

    Sabaqi (Recent Memory): Daily revision of recently learned portions.

    Manzil (Old Memory): Systematic revision of older parts to ensure retention.

  • Tajweed Integration: Apply precise Makharij and pronunciation rules while memorizing.
  • Retentive Memory Techniques: Practical strategies to enhance focus and recall capacity.
